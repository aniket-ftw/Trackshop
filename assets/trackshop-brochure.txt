TRACKSHOP – GA4 PRACTICE STORE

A demo store for learning ecommerce tracking.
Browse six fake products, add items to your cart, and complete a pretend checkout.
Use TRACK10 for a 10% demo discount.

Open the Analytics Debug Panel to inspect local events.

Practice: view_item_list, select_item, view_item, add_to_cart, remove_from_cart, view_cart, begin_checkout, add_shipping_info, add_payment_info, purchase.
